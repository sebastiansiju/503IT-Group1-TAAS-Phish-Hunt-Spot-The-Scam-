Research Objective

The purpose of this research was to identify common phishing techniques used against teenagers and to gather evidence that could be incorporated into the educational game Phish Hunt: Spot the Scam. The research also aimed to identify best practices for cyber-security awareness education and accessibility requirements for users aged 14–16.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers attempt to trick users into revealing personal information such as passwords, bank details, or login credentials by pretending to be a trusted organisation or individual.

Common phishing methods include:

Fake emails pretending to be from trusted companies.
Fraudulent text messages (Smishing).
Social media scams.
Fake prize or giveaway messages.
Urgent account verification requests.
Fake delivery notifications.

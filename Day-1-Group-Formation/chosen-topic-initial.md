## Reason for Selecting This Topic

Phishing Awareness was selected because it is highly relevant to students and young internet users. It helps learners identify common online scams, protect personal information, and develop safe online habits. The topic is also well-suited for an educational game because phishing scenarios can be presented interactively, allowing players to practice recognising and responding to threats in a realistic environment.

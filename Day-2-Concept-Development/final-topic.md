# Day 2: Final Project Topic Selection

## Project Title
**Phishing Awareness & Digital Resilience for Teens**

## Final Chosen Topic
The selected topic for this project is **Phishing Awareness**, specifically tailored to educate and protect teenagers against modern social engineering tactics. 

## Topic Description
Phishing has evolved from obvious, poorly written emails into highly sophisticated, multi-channel attacks including **Smishing** (SMS phishing), **Vishing** (voice phishing), and targeted social media scams. This project explores the mechanisms behind phishing, how attackers exploit human psychology (urgency, curiosity, fear), and how to identify indicators of deception. 

Instead of general corporate training, this project translates cybersecurity principles into concepts that resonate with a younger audience, focusing on platforms they use daily: Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Discord, and online gaming environments (e.g., Roblox, Steam).

## Core Learning Objectives
1. **Identify Vulnerabilities:** Understand what makes an online message, link, or login page suspicious.
2. **Recognize Psychological Triggers:** Learn how scammers use high-pressure tactics (e.g., "Your account will be deleted in 2 hours!").
3. **Analyze Multi-Platform Attacks:** Distinguish between email phishing, SMS scams, and direct message/chat exploits.
4. **Develop Safe Digital Habits:** Establish proactive security behaviors, such as verifying sources out-of-band and utilizing Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

## Relevance and Importance
As digital natives, teenagers spend a significant portion of their lives online, making them prime targets for identity theft, credential harvesting, and account takeovers. Educating this demographic creates a first line of defense that protects not only their personal digital identities but also their families' home networks and future financial footprints.

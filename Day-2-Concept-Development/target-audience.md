# Day 2: Target Audience Analysis

## Defined Demographic
* **Age Range:** 14 to 16 years old
* **Educational Level:** High School / Key Stage 4 (or equivalent)
* **Digital Profile:** Digital natives with high daily screen time, primarily mobile-first users.

## Audience Characteristics & Behavioral Patterns
* **Platform Preferences:** Dominant use of Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Discord, Spotify, and gaming platforms (Steam, Roblox, PlayStation Network). Traditional email is rarely used except for school assignments or creating accounts.
* **Attention Span:** Accustomed to short-form, high-engagement content (vertical videos, quick-disappearing stories).
* **Tech Savviness vs. Security Awareness:** Highly fluent in navigating apps and interfaces, but frequently overconfident regarding digital privacy and cybersecurity risks.
* **Common Online Activities:** Socializing via direct messages, sharing media, online gaming, streaming content, and initial entries into online shopping or micro-transactions.

## Vulnerabilities Specific to Ages 14-16
1. **Account Takeovers (Gaming & Social Media):** Highly susceptible to scams promising free in-game currency (e.g., V-Bucks, Robux), rare skins, or account verification badges.
2. **Social Engineering via Peers:** Vulnerable to compromised friend accounts sending links like *"Look who died in an accident, I think you know them..."* or *"Vote for my team in this tournament."*
3. **Lack of Experience with Official Channels:** Difficulty distinguishing between an authentic automated alert from an app developer and a spoofed phishing page.
4. **Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) / Urgency:** Quick to act on limited-time offers, celebrity giveaways, or urgent notices claiming an account is about to be suspended.

## Tailoring Content for this Audience
To ensure the phishing awareness material is effective, the following design principles will be applied:
* **Gamified Elements:** Replace dense text walls with interactive quizzes, interactive scenario simulations ("Spot the Phish"), and scoreboards.
* **Relatable Examples:** Use screenshots of mock Discord DMs, Instagram messages, and SMS alerts rather than traditional corporate corporate banking emails.

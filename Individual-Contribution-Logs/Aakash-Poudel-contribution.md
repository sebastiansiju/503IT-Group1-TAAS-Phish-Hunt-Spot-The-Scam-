
My role within Team TAAS was Research Lead and Evidence Manager for the development of Phish Hunt: Spot the Scam. My primary responsibility was to conduct research, gather evidence, and support the team with accurate information related to phishing awareness, cyber-security education, accessibility requirements, and target audience needs.

One of my main contributions was researching phishing attacks and common scam techniques used on social media, email platforms, and messaging applications. I collected information from credible cyber-security sources and identified examples that could be adapted into realistic game scenarios. This research helped ensure that the educational content within the game reflected real-world cyber threats faced by teenagers aged 14–16.

I also carried out research into the characteristics and learning preferences of the target audience. Based on my findings, I recommended the use of simple language, interactive decision-making challenges, and immediate feedback mechanisms to improve engagement and learning outcomes. These recommendations influenced several aspects of the game's design and educational approach.

Another significant contribution involved researching accessibility requirements. I reviewed WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and shared recommendations with the team regarding colour contrast, readable typography, keyboard navigation, button sizing, and mobile responsiveness. These findings supported the implementation of accessibility features that made the game more inclusive and user-friendly.

As Evidence Manager, I was responsible for maintaining project documentation and supporting the organisation of evidence required for assessment. This included helping to document meeting discussions, project planning activities, testing outcomes, peer feedback, tutor feedback, and contribution records. Maintaining accurate documentation allowed the team to monitor progress effectively and demonstrate evidence of collaboration throughout the project.

I also contributed to discussions regarding technology selection and development planning. Through research and comparison of available tools, I helped support the team's decision to use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript as the primary technologies for developing the prototype. These technologies were selected because they provided flexibility, accessibility, and compatibility across multiple devices.

Throughout the project, I actively participated in team meetings, collaborative discussions, and decision-making processes. I shared research findings with other team members, provided evidence to support design decisions, and helped ensure that the educational objectives remained aligned with the client brief.

The skills I demonstrated during this project included research and analysis, evidence gathering, documentation management, communication, teamwork, critical thinking, and problem-solving. My contribution helped ensure that the game was educationally effective, evidence-based, and suitable for its intended audience.

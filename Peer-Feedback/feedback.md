# Feedback Report

Feedback was collected from Roshan, Krishna, Priskhya, Aakash, Tasnim, and Sebastian after testing the game on different devices.

## Overall Feedback

The testers found the game engaging, easy to use, and accessible across mobile phones, tablets, and laptops. The user interface was simple to understand, and the gameplay worked smoothly without major issues.

## Suggestions for Improvement

### Real-World Scenarios

Some testers suggested incorporating more real-world scenarios and examples to make the game more realistic and educational.

### Background Music

Several testers recommended adding background music to make the gameplay experience more enjoyable and immersive.

### Voice Mode

Testers suggested including a voice mode or voice guidance feature to improve accessibility and user engagement.

### Sound Quality

The game's audio was functional, but testers felt that the sound quality could be improved for a better overall experience.

### Question Timer

A common piece of feedback was that the time limit for answering questions was too short. Some players felt they did not have enough time to fully read and understand certain questions before the timer expired. Increasing the available response time would improve the user experience.

## Future Improvements

The feedback received during testing will be considered in future updates to enhance gameplay, accessibility, and overall user satisfaction.

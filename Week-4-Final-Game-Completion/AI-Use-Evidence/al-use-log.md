---

# 2. How AI Was Used

The team used AI tools as learning assistants and development support tools rather than relying on them to complete the project independently.

### Brainstorming and Scenario Ideas

AI was used to generate ideas for realistic phishing and scam scenarios aimed at teenagers and students, including:

* QR code scams
* Fake gaming rewards
* Suspicious email messages
* Social media phishing attempts

All scenarios were reviewed, edited, and rewritten by the team before being included in the game.

### Coding and Bug Fixing Assistance

AI tools were used to:

* Explain JavaScript concepts
* Help structure gameplay features
* Provide guidance for timers, scoring systems, and difficulty levels
* Assist with debugging coding issues
* Help identify and fix gameplay and UI bugs
* Suggest improvements for code organisation and functionality

All code and bug fixes were reviewed, tested, and modified by the team members before implementation.

### Documentation Support

AI assisted in formatting and drafting project documentation, including:

* Testing records
* Build plans
* Reflection documents
* Presentation planning

The team added all real project information, testing results, and final edits manually.

### Accessibility Guidance

AI was used to provide suggestions regarding:

* Colour contrast
* Readability
* User-friendly UI design

The team reviewed and applied the final accessibility decisions independently.

---

# 3. What AI Was NOT Used For

* AI was not used to complete the entire project automatically.
* AI-generated content was not submitted without review and editing.
* AI was not used to create fake testing results or false documentation.
* Final decisions regarding gameplay, design, coding, and presentation were made by the team members.

# Presentation Plan — Phish Hunt

## Project Information

**Project:** Phish Hunt — Spot the Scam!
**Presentation Length:** 15–20 Minutes
**Individual Speaking Time:** Approximately 5–7 Minutes Per Member
**Date:** 05/28/2026

---

# Goal of the Presentation

The goal of this presentation is to demonstrate the final version of the Phish Hunt game, explain how it teaches cyber-safety awareness to teenagers, showcase the improvements made during development, and discuss the testing, bug fixing, and teamwork involved in completing the project.

The presentation will also explain how the game helps users identify phishing scams through interactive gameplay and educational feedback.
